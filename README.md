One word describing my home Wi-Fi, entered in the [One Word for Your Wi-Fi](https://make.fun/bounty/u-tuan-muda-one-word-for-your-wi-fi-a1b4fd72) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The word

**Flaky.**

My Wi-Fi drops and comes back whenever it feels like it — mid-video, mid-game,
mid-sentence — with no warning and no reason anyone can find. Full bars one
second, nothing the next. It is the most honest single word for a connection
that cannot be counted on.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13) writes that one word to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
# Flaky
```
